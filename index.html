Technology with a purpose

Our modern office is equipped with:

3D CBCT Imaging

  • Creates a detailed 3D view of teeth, roots, bone, sinuses, and nearby anatomy.
  • Supports precise diagnosis and planning for implants, extractions, root canals, and complex care.

TRIOS 5 Intraoral Scanner

  • Captures a highly detailed digital model without conventional impression trays.
  • Improves comfort, supports well-fitting restorations, and makes treatment easier to visualize.

Intraoral Camera

  • Provides magnified, full-color images from inside your mouth.
  • Documents changes and helps you clearly see cracks, wear, and other findings.

Digital X-rays

  • Reveals decay, infection, and bone changes that may not be visible during an exam.
  • Produces clear images quickly with less radiation than traditional film-based X-rays.
