Meet Dr. Suraj Bhogal.

For Dr. Bhogal, dentistry has always been about more than fixing teeth. It’s about understanding the person in the chair, earning their trust, and providing care that can genuinely improve the way they look, feel, and live.

Originally from the East Coast, Dr. Bhogal earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston before eventually making the move to the Pacific Northwest. Today, he is proud to call the Seattle area home and to serve the Renton community as a practice owner.

Dr. Bhogal has a particular passion for implant, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry. From rebuilding a single tooth to transforming an entire smile, he enjoys cases that combine thoughtful treatment planning, modern technology, and attention to detail. His clinical experience includes dental implants, immediate implant placement, complex extractions, root canal therapy, crowns, veneers, and comprehensive smile rehabilitation.

A lifelong learner by nature, Dr. Bhogal is constantly investing in advanced continuing education and new techniques to expand what he can offer his patients. His goal is simple: whenever possible, he wants patients to receive high-quality, comprehensive treatment from a dentist they already know and trust.

Just as important as the dentistry itself is how patients feel while receiving it. Dr. Bhogal believes great care starts with listening. He takes the time to explain treatment, answer questions, and involve patients in decisions about their health rather than simply telling them what they “need.” His approach is relaxed, honest, and centered around building relationships that last well beyond a single appointment.

Outside the office, Dr. Bhogal believes strongly in personal growth and enjoying life outside of work. You’ll usually find him staying active, playing guitar, traveling somewhere new, searching for great food and coffee, or indulging his enthusiasm for cars.

Most of all, he considers it a privilege to have built a practice where he can combine the two things he values most in dentistry: doing exceptional work and taking exceptional care of people.
